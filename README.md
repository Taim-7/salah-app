# Salah

Salah is a prayer timetable for the UK and Ireland. It shows local prayer times, the Qibla direction, fasting records and Islamic dates. Its iPhone Home Screen version can use the local timetable, Qibla and Learn offline after the first visit. New installs use Dayline and Midnight Navy. The logo in the header follows the selected icon and colours.

The timetable uses a 12-hour clock. The red Forbidden rows show when to avoid general voluntary prayer. The night runs from Maghrib to the next Fajr. The timetable marks the first third and the selected end of Isha, and shows the preferred Isha window when it is available. [Ibn ‘Uthaymin’s explanation](https://old.binothaimeen.net/content/11812)

The app starts in English. You can change the language to Arabic in Settings, including place names and GPS labels. In English lessons and date details, Arabic verses and narrations appear above their English meanings. The Dates tab includes Ramadan fasting and night prayer, the two Eids, Zakat al-Fitr, optional fasts and other worship. The Hijri dates are estimates. Follow local crescent announcements for observances.

The Qibla compass makes soft ticks as you turn closer and plays a distinct chime once the heading settles within 2°. Vibration depends on the device and browser. Dimashqiya is included as an offline backup for prayer-time adhan. Scheduled sound in Safari or the iPhone Home Screen app needs Salah to stay visible and the browser to allow audio for the session.
