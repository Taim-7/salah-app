# Salah

Salah is a prayer timetable for the UK and Ireland. It shows local prayer times, the Qibla direction, fasting records and Islamic dates. Its iPhone Home Screen version can use the local timetable, Qibla and Learn offline after the first visit. New installs use Dayline and Midnight Navy. The logo in the header follows the selected icon and colours.

The timetable uses a 12-hour clock. Upcoming times when voluntary prayer is forbidden appear in red. The night runs from Maghrib to the next Fajr. The timetable marks the first third and the selected end of Isha, and shows the preferred Isha window when it is available. [Ibn ‘Uthaymin’s explanation](https://old.binothaimeen.net/content/11812)

The app starts in English and has an Arabic option in Settings. In English lessons and date details, Arabic verses and narrations appear above their English meanings. The Hijri dates are estimates and local crescent announcements determine the actual observances.

The Qibla compass makes soft ticks as you turn closer and plays a distinct chime once the heading settles within 2°. Vibration depends on the device and browser. Dimashqiya is included with the website. Scheduled sound in Safari or the iPhone Home Screen app needs Salah to stay visible and the browser to allow audio for the session.
