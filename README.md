# Salah

Salah is a prayer timetable for the UK and Ireland, with Derry as its starting location. It works in a browser and can be added to an iPhone’s Home Screen. The local timetable, nearby town search and Qibla bearing work offline after the app has loaded and cached its files.

## What it does

The timetable shows the five prayers, sunrise, the three short forbidden periods and the night markers. The clock follows the selected town. The first third and halfway point run from sunset to the following Fajr.

Derry starts at 15° Fajr and 13° Isha, with one-shadow Asr and a one-seventh fallback for missing crossings. Use **Settings → When your angle has no crossing** to choose one seventh, an angle fraction, local relative estimation, last valid day or the seasonal Tayba reference. **Crossings only** leaves a missing Fajr or Isha blank. The comparison shows the same day under each method. The [local relative calculation](https://astronomycenter.net/latitude.html?l=en) uses annual local twilight fractions for each angle and five-minute steps at either end of the missing period. Last valid day keeps the clock time from the last crossing. [Angle fraction and one seventh](https://praytimes.org/docs/calculation) use the local sunset-to-sunrise interval.

## Using it

For a local preview, run `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder and open `http://localhost:8000`. For Home Screen installation, serve the folder over HTTPS, open the address in Safari, then use **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Open it online once before using it offline.

Sounds can play while the app is open, subject to the browser’s audio settings. Abu Bakr’s Fajr recording is paired with the regular recording for other prayers. The recordings come from [alsuleiman-lab](https://github.com/alsuleiman-lab/athkar-audio) and are used with the owner’s permission. The Calendar export starts from the displayed date and includes 15-minute alerts. A local export covers up to 60 days and an AlAdhan export covers the selected day. Import the file into a calendar that accepts `.ics` files. Export again if you change a prayer setting or move to another town.

## Accuracy

True dawn is the light that spreads along the eastern horizon. It marks both the start of Fajr and the start of fasting. Red twilight fading in the west marks Isha in the approach taken by al-Shafi‘i in *al-Umm* and discussed by Ibn Taymiyyah. A solar depression angle is a model for these signs. It tells you where the Sun is, while the way the light looks also depends on the atmosphere and location. The [OpenFajr study](https://openfajr.org/docs/research_paper.pdf) used photographs from Birmingham. Tayba draws on work in Leicester. Both projects used locations in England. [Qur’an 2:187](https://quran.com/2/187) · [Abu Dawud 2348](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2348) · [Al-Umm, Isha](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/31/223/) · [Ibn Taymiyyah](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/2509/)

Asr starts when an object's shadow has lengthened by one object height after the noon shadow. The Isha setting offers the first third as described in *al-Umm*, the halfway cutoff as explained by Ibn ‘Uthaymin, and Ibn Baz’s separate necessity period between halfway and Fajr. Salah omits an Isha time that overlaps the following Fajr. Night markers depend on a usable next Fajr. [Al-Umm, Asr](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/31/220/) · [Sahih Muslim 612](https://sunnah.com/muslim:612d) · [Ibn ‘Uthaymin on Isha](https://old.binothaimeen.net/content/12502) · [Ibn Baz on Isha](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/8293/)

The forbidden-time rows are for the three short periods described by ‘Uqbah ibn ‘Amir. The sunrise and sunset ranges use the Sun’s centre at 4° above the geometric horizon as an estimate of a spear’s height. The midday range starts eight minutes before solar noon as a precaution and ends when the Sun has passed solar noon. The wider restriction on general voluntary prayer starts after you perform Fajr or Asr. Ibn ‘Uthaymin discusses exceptions for prayers with a specific reason. [Sahih Muslim 831](https://sunnah.com/muslim:831) · [Sahih al-Bukhari 586](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:586) · [Ibn ‘Uthaymin on the forbidden times](https://old.binothaimeen.net/content/11596) · [Ishraq altitude study](https://doi.org/10.21580/al-hilal.2025.7.2.27963)

Sunrise and sunset use the visible upper edge of the Sun at a standard sea-level horizon. The calculation models that with the Sun’s centre 0.8333° below the geometric horizon. A hill, an elevated viewpoint or unusual refraction can shift what you see locally. Prayer starts round up to the next minute and sunrise rounds down. Dhuhr includes one minute after solar transit before rounding. For Qibla, the bearing is clockwise from true north. A compatible iPhone heading is corrected using the bundled WMM 2025 model, valid from 2025 through 2029. You can also enter a local magnetic correction. [US Naval Observatory definitions](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/RST_defs) · [World Magnetic Model](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model)

## Timetable sources

[Tayba option 2](https://taybatrust.org/index.php/projects/establishing-dawn/34-dp-all-loc) uses 14.5° for Fajr outside its summer interval. During that interval it uses a fixed gap before sunrise derived from the last spring date with an 18° crossing. Its Isha estimate uses 14.5° or 90 minutes after sunset subject to the 8° crossing. When that 8° crossing is unavailable, the Isha cell is blank. Choose the full Tayba model in Compare, or choose **Seasonal Tayba reference** in Settings to use it only for a missing crossing. Tayba’s data comes from Leicester. The hybrid method applies it to the selected town as an estimate. [Ibn ‘Uthaymin on late and absent twilight](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/135415)

[AlAdhan’s Moonsighting preset](https://aladhan.com/prayer-times-api) supplies Fajr and Isha when selected. The main timetable uses the same local sunlight calculation for the other times. Compare shows the provider's full raw table. Saved online times can be reused offline. If a date has no saved AlAdhan times, Salah shows the local replacement and leaves it out of online-source reminders. The prayer preset is separate from lunar-month announcements. For congregation times, see the [North West Islamic Centre timetable](https://waqt.uk/northwestislamiccentre/) and the [Letterkenny and Donegal timetables](https://islaminletterkenny.com/).

When checking a local twilight time, record the date, place and horizon conditions, and note when the dawn light starts to spread or the evening redness disappears. Observations from a clear horizon on different dates can be checked against the computed angles. [OpenFajr methodology](https://openfajr.org/docs/research_paper.pdf)

Offline location checks use [Natural Earth’s UK and Ireland boundaries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/), with a one-kilometre allowance near the coastline. For a GPS position outside the built-in towns, BigDataCloud can supply a place name when online. The saved coordinates determine the time zone. The magnetic model implementation comes from [magvar 2.2.0](https://github.com/dpyeates/magvar).
