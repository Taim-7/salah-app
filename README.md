# Salah

Salah is a prayer timetable for the UK and Ireland. The website runs in Safari and as an iPhone Home Screen app. After the first visit, it can calculate local prayer times and the direction of Qibla offline when the device supplies its location. The fasting log stays on the device. The Islamic dates and Learn lessons are also available offline, with lessons in English and Arabic.

The Qibla compass shows how far to turn and goes green when the reported heading is within 2° of Qibla. True and magnetic bearings are available for use with other compasses.

Salah measures the night from Maghrib to the next Fajr. When Isha has begun and the timetable allows the preferred window, it says **Preferred time for Isha in 10 minutes** before the first third of the night passes. This follows the view that delaying Isha until between one third and halfway through the night is preferred when possible. The message instead describes the cutoff if the first third is set as the end of Isha. [Ibn ‘Uthaymin’s explanation](https://old.binothaimeen.net/content/11812)

With Salah open and visible, the chosen adhan or chime can play at a prayer start or at the Isha reminder after **Play sound** starts audio for the session. Salah can send an audible phone notification when it cannot start the in-app sound, if notifications are allowed. The website cannot schedule alerts or play its adhan while an iPhone is locked or Salah is closed. Damascus is available offline after the website has cached the recording.
