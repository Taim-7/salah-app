ABOUT THE CALCULATIONS

What these times actually mean

✳
Why are there two Isha ending opinions?+

Al-Nasa’i records the Prophet instructing people to pray Isha from the disappearance of twilight until one third of the night. Sahih Muslim 612 states that Isha lasts until half of the night. Hanbali authorities differed about the end of the chosen or preferred time, with another period of necessity for genuine excuses said to last until Fajr. Some scholars instead take half-night as the final ordinary ending. The app shows both milestones and labels your selected approach; it does not declare the other opinion invalid.

Why is Isha based on red twilight rather than a fixed angle?+

Hanbali fiqh links the start of Isha to disappearance of red twilight. A solar depression angle is only a prediction, and it does not directly measure the colour and visibility of twilight. The 12°/12° French UOIF calculation is a timetable convention; no fixed angle can certify the religious event at every location and season.

What happens in the long summer twilight?+

In northern summers the Sun may never descend to your selected angle. The app marks missing crossings as estimates and applies your selected sunset-to-sunrise fraction. Some places may retain a red glow well into the night. No high-latitude fraction proves the observation of true dawn or red twilight; compare the published methods and seek scholarly guidance on the chosen estimation rule.

How does the Hanbali method calculate Asr?+

The Hanbali / majority one-shadow method starts Asr once the extra shadow beyond the solar-noon shadow equals the object height. This is not the two-shadow Hanafi time. The earlier preferred Asr period ends when the sunlight yellows, which cannot be set to an exact fixed clock time merely by geometry.

Are all prayers forbidden in the displayed periods?+

These restrictions concern voluntary prayers, with exceptions and differences over reason-based prayers and missed obligations. After Asr, the restriction begins when you have personally prayed Asr. After Fajr there are differences: many start it after Fajr prayer, while a traditional Hanbali presentation begins at true dawn with exceptions. The short sunrise, zenith and near-sunset periods are clock precautions, not exact religious boundaries. Do not treat a displayed estimated minute as a certain ruling.

References and accuracy+

Sahih Muslim 612 — half-night and prayer boundaries; Sunan an-Nasa’i 535 — first-third hadith; Hanbali Isha preferred/necessity times; Ibn Qudamah and Hanbali variation; UOIF France 12°/12° method; Prayer restrictions and sunrise precaution; Astronomical sunrise definition: USNO; NOAA solar calculation explanation; Difference between angles and observed dawn

The calculations use the Sun’s predicted position with sea-level refraction and horizon assumptions. Sunrise, Asr and solar noon can vary by a few minutes from other methods. Fajr and Isha angles and high-latitude adjustments are estimates, not certain religious observations. Elevation and obstructed horizons can change observations. Islamic dates are approximate. The app supports optional foreground chimes and calendar-event exports, but not background adhan or web push without a notification service.

When on, Salah sends the selected coordinates and date to the AlAdhan API. Turn it off to use only offline methods and previously saved results. No account is required. Press Refresh online for a one-time lookup.

Fajr and Isha angles

These depression angles are astronomical predictions. Neither 12° nor 13° nor 15° guarantees the appearance of true dawn or disappearance of red twilight.

French UOIF / Musulmans de France is generally described as 12°/12°. The other presets are separate settings, not French UOIF.

When does the normal Isha time end?

There are two scholarly approaches in these sources. The first third is commonly the better time to finish Isha. Some Hanbali works end the preferred period then. Sahih Muslim states the Isha time extends to half the night; some later time is only a necessity allowance in the Hanbali school.

High-latitude estimates

If the Sun never reaches your chosen twilight angle, the app estimates a time from the night between sunset and sunrise. This affects summer in Derry.

This fallback is used only when the angle is not reached, and the affected times are clearly marked.
