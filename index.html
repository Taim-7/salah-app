      Location

      Fajr and Isha angles

      Angles estimate the religious signs. A missing crossing tells you about the Sun’s position.

      Isha

      The night runs from sunset to the next Fajr.

      When your angle has no crossing

      An existing crossing stays in use. A missing crossing only tells you about the Sun’s position. The estimate cannot establish what the local sky looked like.

      How the methods differ

      One seventh and angle fraction use the local interval between sunset and sunrise.

      Local relative uses annual local twilight fractions and five-minute steps at either end of the period. Last valid day carries forward the last crossing’s clock time.

      Seasonal Tayba estimates the missing prayer using Tayba. Crossings only leaves the time blank.
