PERSONALISE

Settings

Appearance

Choose a colour and timetable view.

Location

Find a UK or Ireland city, or use your iPhone’s location.

GPS tracking works only while the web app is active, not continually in the background. With internet access, it sends your chosen coordinates to BigDataCloud (city name) and Open-Meteo (time zone). Times still calculate offline.

Online comparison

When on, Salah sends the selected coordinates and date to the AlAdhan API. Turn it off to pause other comparisons. A selected online source still needs internet access. No account required.

Fajr and Isha angles

Preferred Isha timing

Isha starts after red twilight. The selected preference changes the first-third marker; halfway is shown separately.

High-latitude estimates

If the Sun never reaches your chosen twilight angle, the app estimates a time from the night between sunset and sunrise. This affects summer in Derry.

Estimated dates are marked in the timetable.
